Quiz me on what we built in this session: 5 interview-style questions
(2 concepts, 2 about our design decisions, 1 "what would break if...").
Ask ONE question at a time and wait for my answer.
After each answer: score it out of 10, say what was right, fill the gaps in plain words,
and ask me to restate it in one sentence if I scored below 7.
At the end: summarize my strong and weak spots, and list the weak spots as
3 short bullets I can copy into my private notes.
